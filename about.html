About

Frankie Seratch is an actor based in New York and Los Angeles.

He stars in RECLUSE, the new feature from Henry Chaisson (Antlers, Servant), having its world premiere at the 2026 Tribeca Film Festival. His thriller Human Resource, opposite Veanne Cox, is on the festival circuit. He plays opposite Dolly Lewis in Bela Baptiste’s The Obelisk, and was directed by Ben Affleck in a Super Bowl campaign for Dunkin’ opposite Druski.

He appears alongside Mike Faist in The Grief of Others, which premiered at SXSW and Cannes, and played opposite David Hyde Pierce at the Vineyard Theatre.

Studies with Lesly Kahn (Los Angeles) and Jon Shear, Ted Sluberski, Guy Stroman (New York).
